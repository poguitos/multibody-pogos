# Phase 7: the road

- **Dates:** 24 November 2026
- **Plan:** task 7.2; D42 (the ISO 8608 roads), D61
- **Commits:** see git log
- **Written:** at the time

## Goal

Task 7.2: "Road. One interface returning height, normal and friction at a
point. Flat, analytic (ramp, bump, kerb, sine), gridded height map, and a
three-dimensional track surface. ISO 8608 random roads, classes A to H,
generated from the standard's displacement spectrum with a seed (D42). *Done
when the tyre uses only this interface, and a generated ISO 8608 road
reproduces its class's spectrum.*"

## Starting point

After task 7.1 the two tyres (`FullTireForce`, the Pacejka tyre of the
vehicles; `TireContactForce`, the vertical one of the quarter car) met a flat
plane: a height and a normal, set by the builders from gravity. The lap tools
had a `Track`: a centreline in the ground plane, by arc length, with
elevation, slope and banking per segment. The tyre law had a friction
coefficient but no road to scale it.

## What was done

1. **The interface** (`mbd/forces/road.hpp`, D61): `Road::at(x, y)` gives a
   `RoadPoint` (height, unit normal, friction), ISO 8855 axes. Friction is a
   scale on the tyre's own; in the tyre law it multiplies the peak D and
   leaves the slip stiffness K = BCD.
2. **The roads:** flat; ramp, bump (raised cosine), kerb (a chamfered strip
   along the road), sine; a gridded height map (bilinear, its edges continuing
   level); ISO 8608 (a sum of cosines at n_k = k / L with A_k =
   sqrt(2 G_d(n_k) / L) and seeded phases, sampled and interpolated by
   Catmull-Rom cubics); and the track surface (`mbd/analysis/track_surface.hpp`),
   projecting a point onto the centreline through a grid of buckets and
   Newton on the arc length.
3. **The tyres** hold a road, level at height 0 unless given another, and take
   everything about the ground from it: the depth below its tangent plane at
   the contact point's (x, y), (h - z) n_z; the force along its normal; the
   heading and slips in its tangent plane; the friction into the law. A null
   road is refused (MBD-F051). The builders lost their tyre normals and
   gained `set_road`.
4. **Tests:** each road against its closed form and its normal against
   central differences of its heights; the gridded road on planes and nodes;
   ISO 8608's table; every class's generated road against its spectrum; a
   tyre on a bump's flank and on a ramp; the road's friction on the tyre's
   peak and stiffness; the track surface on a banked straight, a banked
   turn, a climbing turn and a closed oval; the sedan over a ramp; and a
   regression scenario, the sedan on an ISO 8608 road of class C.

## Decisions

D61. Smaller ones: the road lives with the tyre in `mbd/forces`, the track
surface with the track in `mbd/analysis` (so `forces` needs nothing from
`analysis`); one message code for every road's parameters (MBD-F050), as
MBD-F040 serves every contact parameter; the regression sensitivity probe now
prints each scenario's worst channel at 1e-12.

## What went wrong, and how it was found

- **A regression scenario that measured rounding.** The first version of
  `sedan-rough-road` drove straight. The sensitivity probe said a change of
  1e-12 in gravity moved it by 1.8e8 times its tolerance. Printing the worst
  channel (added to the probe for this) named the roll rate: an ISO 8608 road
  is the same across its width, so a straight-driving car on it is
  symmetric, its roll, yaw and lateral motion zero but for rounding, and a
  tolerance of 1e-8 of rounding is no tolerance. The scenario now steers
  after 0.5 s: 6.2e-4 and 616, like the others. The README says why such
  channels are avoided.
- **A test's expectation, not the code.** The track surface's oval test
  expected a point 1 m before the start, 2 m to the left, to project 1 m
  before the end and 2 m off the centreline; but the oval ends on its
  closing half circle, not on a straight. The code's values (1.0343 m
  before the end, 1.99138 m inside the turn) are the closed form,
  60 atan(1/58) and 60 - sqrt(1 + 58^2); the test now asserts those.
- **The message catalogue edited after the suite had started.** The new
  codes were added to `docs/help/messages.md` while the build ran; the
  catalogue test had already run and failed. It passed when run again, and
  the whole suite then passed. Edit the files tests read before starting the
  suite, as with sources.
- **Escapes in scripted edits again:** a `\n` inside a replacement string
  became a newline; the script's own check stopped it before anything was
  written, and the editor was used instead. (The same happened to the first
  attempt at writing this paragraph.)

## Verification

- **ISO 8608, every class A to H** (`test_road.cpp`): a 200 m road per class
  (seed 1000 + class), its 5,660 samples read through `at()`, the
  periodogram by the DFT's definition in the test. In the band (0.011 to
  2.83 cycles/m) every frequency holds G_d(n) = G_d(n0) (n / n0)^-2 to
  5.3e-13 to 6.5e-13 relative (allowed 1e-9); out of it at most 1.1e-25 of
  the smallest in-band value (allowed 1e-12); at n0 = 0.1 cycles/m each
  class's G is its table value. Same seed, same road; another seed, another
  road; one period later, the same height.
- **The tyre uses only the road:** its code reads nothing of the ground but
  `road->at()`. On flat ground the change is exact: every vehicle regression
  scenario reproduces its reference with a difference of 0.
- **Closed forms:** ramp, bump, kerb, sine, plane-on-a-grid heights and
  normals to 1e-14 or better; normals against central differences within
  1e-8 everywhere they are smooth; a tyre on a bump's flank pushes
  k 0.005 n_z along the normal to 1e-9 relative; on a road of friction 0.5 the
  tyre's peak lateral force is 2000 N (4000 N on 1.0), 5.7e-4 N and 3.8e-5 N
  from the closed form D = mu Fz times the friction (allowed 0.02 and
  0.04 N; the wet one's shortfall is the sampling's, 2.9e-7 relative against
  4e-7 derived), and its cornering stiffness 45000 N/rad on both, 2.6e-13
  relative apart; the sedan over a 5 cm ramp ends
  0.049993 m higher than on the level (allowed 1e-4 m from 0.05).
- **The track surface:** points placed at known (s, d) project back to them
  within 1e-10 m, heights z_c - d tan(bank) to 1e-10 m, normals against
  differences within 1e-8.
- **The suite:** 563 tests pass (546 before; 17 new), the build without a
  warning, the laptop on battery. The regression sensitivity probe passes for
  every scenario.

## Lessons

- **A regression channel needs a magnitude.** A tolerance relative to a
  channel's largest value is no tolerance where that value is rounding.
- **Check a test's expectation before the code** when the code's value has a
  closed form of its own.

## Open ends

- The contact point is still fixed in the wheel's frame (F8, task 7.3); on a
  curved road the tyre meets the road at the point below the wheel's centre
  in the wheel's own frame, not at the nearest point of the road.
- The aerodynamics measure ride height above flat ground, not above the road.
- An ISO 8608 road is the same across its width; two tracks with a coherence
  between them are for the four-post rig (9.4).
- Banking changes in steps between the track's segments.
