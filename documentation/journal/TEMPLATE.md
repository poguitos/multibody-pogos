# Title: what was done, in a few words

- **Dates:** when the work began and ended
- **Plan:** task numbers, and the findings or decisions involved
- **Commits:** hashes
- **Written:** at the time, or reconstructed (from what)

## Goal

What the task was meant to achieve, and its "done when" condition.

## Starting point

The state of the code and of our understanding before the work.

## What was done

In the order it happened, including the steps that were undone.

## Decisions

Each choice with its alternatives and the reason for it. Larger ones go to
`documentation/decisions.md`; refer to them by number here.

## What went wrong, and how it was found

Defects, wrong hypotheses, misleading measurements, tooling trouble. How each
was noticed, and how it was resolved.

## Verification

The tests added or changed, what each proves, and the derivation behind every
tolerance.

## Measurements

Each number with its method and machine state; raw output in `data/`.

## Lessons

What to keep doing, and what to do differently.

## Open ends

What is left, and where it is tracked.

## For the book

Candidate figures, derivations worth writing out, examples that explain an
idea well, and the chapter of `documentation/book/outline.md` this belongs to.
