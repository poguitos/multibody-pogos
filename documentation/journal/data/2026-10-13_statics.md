# Static equilibrium: diagnosis, margins and timings, 13 October 2026

Code: branch `phase-3`, the commit of task 3.5 unless stated. Laptop
i7-13700H, 16 GB, Windows 11, MSVC 19.50, RelWithDebInfo, one compile job.

## 1. The diagnosis: a rank of 6 where there were 7

First version of statics, with `RankRevealingLDLT` built on Eigen's `LDLT`.
The double-wishbone sedan from `set_vehicle_equilibrium` and `initialize()`.
Temporary prints in the Newton loop (removed), and a temporary hidden test
(`scratch_statics_debug.cpp`, not committed) that computed the statics
Jacobian G the way statics does and took its SVD.

Newton, as it ran:

| Iteration | rank reported | largest acceleration | \|step\| | \|G step + a\| (linear prediction) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7 | 73.4 | 1.49e-3 | 1.178 |
| 2 | 6 | 1.178 | 3.4e-11 | 1.178 |
| 3 to 5 | 6 | 1.178 | about 1e-11 | 1.178 |

After iteration 1 the residual was the chassis's vertical acceleration alone
(1.178 m/s^2; every other component below 3e-9), all four tyres loaded at
4123.4 N, and the heave column of G smooth and linear (da4/dq4 = -73.48 for
steps of 1e-3 to 1e-7). The SVD of the same G at that point:

    1.903e+05 1.426e+05 1.139e+05 2.190e+04 2.556e+01 1.296e+01 7.848e+00 | 1.834e-06 1.227e-06 ...

seven directions with stiffness, then a gap of seven orders. Scaled as
statics scales it (S G S^-1, S the square root of the diagonal of M), and in
force form (S^-1 M G S^-1):

    S G S^-1:       1.371e+04 1.354e+04 1.311e+04 1.277e+04 1.240e+02 1.057e+02 5.005e+01 | 2.666e-06 ...
    S^-1 M G S^-1:  1.173e+04 1.155e+04 1.095e+04 1.050e+04 1.071e+02 9.379e+01 4.732e+01 | 2.343e-06 ...

the seventh squared, against the first squared, 1.3e-5 and 1.6e-5: far
above the factorization's cut of 1e-10. Asymmetry |A - A^T| / |A| of the
two scaled forms at the start: 0.98 and 2.2e-3. A pure heave acceleration
lay in the range of G to 1.2e-3. So G was right and the factorization of
Gs Gs^T was wrong: Eigen's LDLT, which is not rank-revealing, eliminated past
the seventh pivot into rounding error of order 1e-12 and divided by it.

With the factorization stopped at the numerical rank, the same start:

    Largest acceleration at rest: 7.34e+01 -> 3.06e-12; 4 Newton iterations
    (1.79e+01, 1.04e-01, 6.23e-06, 3.06e-12); 0 relaxation steps

## 2. The folded suspension

The sedan raised 5 cm (then 3 cm) before statics: largest acceleration at
rest 1.02e+03 at the start (the wheels in the air, sprung by 4000 N of
preload). It converged, to an equilibrium 0.357 m lower than the reference,
the car pitched 0.26 rad, the rear lower arms turned -1.479 rad and the rear
uprights about 3.0 rad. With the step limits, from 3 cm: 14 Newton
iterations, the residual falling 906, 848, 786, 721, 651, 576, 492, 393,
239, 49.9, 13.1, 13.1, 8.8e-6, 2.2e-11 as the arm walked down 10 degrees an
iteration to the same folded state. Before the limits and with relaxation
driven by the full dynamics, the 5 cm start ran out its 20000 relaxation
steps at 4.4 m/s^2 with the chassis 0.167 m forward and pitched 0.227 rad.
