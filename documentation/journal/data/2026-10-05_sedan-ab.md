joint, steering rack, four corners closed by 17 constraint equations, springs,
dampers, tyres, drivetrain at 30 % throttle after 0.2 s). Microseconds per
call, median of seven rounds. "Old" is commit `24faade` (4 October), extracted
with `git archive` and built separately against the cached dependency sources;
its benchmark has a different row layout.
