## 2. Compile times

From `build/.ninja_log` (end minus start, ms), and for single files the
compile command from `ninja -t commands <object>` run with `/Bt+` in place
of `/showIncludes` and the object written to a scratch folder.

First version, the least change through Eigen's
`CompleteOrthogonalDecomposition<MatX>`:

| File | In the build | Alone, wall | Front end (c1xx) | Back end (c2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `src/kernel/assembly.cpp` | 39.5 s | 37.0 s | 25.9 s | 10.8 s |
| `tests/kernel/test_kernel_assembly.cpp` | 57.6 s, 60.2 s | 46.9 s | 33.3 s | 13.3 s |
| the same `assembly.cpp` with the decomposition stubbed out | | 5.6 s | 1.5 s | 3.8 s |

The precompiled header already includes `<Eigen/Dense>` (so the QR module), so
the cost is the instantiation of the decomposition and its solve, not
parsing.

Final version, the least change through `RankRevealingLDLT`, whose members
are compiled once in `constrained_dynamics.cpp`, and the test's references
written on the planar loop equations with 2 x 2 inverses:

| File | In the full rebuild |
|---|---|
| `src/kernel/assembly.cpp` | 9.4 s |
| `tests/kernel/test_kernel_assembly.cpp` | 10.4 s |
| `tests/vehicle/test_vehicle_assembly.cpp` | 1.6 s |
| `src/kernel/constrained_dynamics.cpp` | 13.7 s |

Full rebuild after the change to `constrained_dynamics.hpp` (both
precompiled headers rebuilt): 85 objects, 498.6 s = 8.3 min wall at one job
(7.4 min for 65 objects on 5 October). Slowest objects: `test_spatial.cpp`
50.8 s, `test_kernel_constraints.cpp` 34.4 s, the test precompiled header
29.2 s, `test_force_library.cpp` 28.0 s, `test_kernel_joints_physics.cpp`
27.7 s.
